# Trap Tails M1 artwork

The atlas and background were generated for Trap Tails using the built-in ImageGen tool from text prompts. The generated images used no reference screenshot as an input. The flag, signs, platform marks and pose animation were authored in the feature code. The catalogue thumbnail is a crop of the running prototype. The external reference library is private study material and is absent from the repository and game bundle.

| File                                     | Source and export                                                                       | Runtime role              |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| `public/games/trap-tails/orchard.webp`   | Rusty Orchard background plate, 1648 × 928 lossy WebP, 159,414 bytes                    | Distant scenery           |
| `public/games/trap-tails/cutouts.webp`   | Mechanical, landmark and Miso cutouts, 1024 × 1024 lossy WebP with alpha, 228,306 bytes | Three.js texture atlas    |
| `public/games/trap-tails/thumbnail.webp` | Browser screenshot crop, 640 × 360 lossy WebP, 33,214 bytes                             | Catalogue card and splash |
| `docs/trap-tails/art/miso-rig.ora`       | Layered Miso OpenRaster document for Krita or compatible editors                        | Editable pose artwork     |

Runtime atlas rectangles are in `src/features/trap-tails/atlas.json`. The eight rig layers, pivots, scales, and expression swap are in `rig.ts`. Art scale, sign placement, and depth are set in `render.ts`. Collision uses separately authored shapes in `content.ts` and `movement.ts`.

## ImageGen prompt set

- **Background.** Wide 16:9 original 2D parallax background plate for RUSTY ORCHARD, an independent storybook platform game. A neglected fruit-packing yard beneath the Catskill hills at late afternoon just after rain. Entire image is scenery far behind the gameplay plane: low contrast distant lavender ridges, hand-painted uneven watercolor clouds, tiny squat copper-roofed cider mill toward left, hazy apple tree rows, faint wires, muted peach-pink wash high in sky, slate plum base and hazy amber horizon. Big calm open sky occupying upper 68%, scenic buildings restricted to bottom third, no large objects at edges, no foreground, no playable platforms, no characters, no game UI, no text. Tactile vintage children's book gouache and screenprinted paper with uneven edges and carefully controlled saturation. Horizontal front-on wide composition. A complete understated 16:9 illustration.
- **Mechanical props.** Original game asset sprite sheet showing four separate 2D hand-painted mechanical props, arranged precisely in four equal quadrants (2 columns × 2 rows) on a genuine transparent background. For an indie storybook platform game set in a derelict cider packing yard. Top left: a long rough straight horizontal plank of old honey-colored timber, thin, frontal side-on view with darker underside, knots and iron bolts, flat uninterrupted top edge. Top right: a square dark purple wrought iron lattice girder with visible X-brace and rivets, front-on, straight edges. Bottom left: an isolated perfectly circular copper and iron spinning saw blade sprite, amber steel teeth and plum hub, flat front view, nothing attached. Bottom right: a squat horizontal mechanical conveyor side view, black desaturated plum belt with two clearly visible circular brass rollers, rectangular top surface, scratched steel. Leave ample transparent padding inside each quadrant and strong gaps between assets. Gouache on rough paper and vintage illustrated industrial toy palette with ink details. No text, no shadows outside cutouts, no human or animal, no scene, all transparent except the four distinct matte objects.
- **Miso rig.** 2D game character cutout parts sprite sheet for an entirely original vintage storybook raccoon named Miso, a cider-yard mechanic wearing a jade teal bandana. True transparent background. Exactly 6 detached sprite pieces, in a 3 column by 2 row equally spaced grid, each component fully contained in its own cell with large empty padding on all sides and no overlap. Row 1 column 1: large head alone facing right three-quarter view, grey fur, triangular ears, toothless grin, dark fur mask and ivory cheeks. Row 1 column 2: small pear-shaped slate grey torso alone facing right with jade bandana at the top edge and cream belly, no appendages. Row 1 column 3: long curved bushy bluegrey striped raccoon tail, sweeping to the left of the narrower base at far right. Row 2 column 1: one short tapered grey arm with charcoal paw, vertical shoulder top. Row 2 column 2: one slate grey leg and charcoal boot-like furry foot pointing right, vertical hip top. Row 2 column 3: a variant of the head with round alarmed eyes and frowning mouth. Matte detailed watercolor gouache and rough paper edge, warm shaded tinted ink, legible silhouettes at tiny scale. No text, no cell borders, no scene, no other props. This sprite sheet is for a mechanical pivot rig, pieces must be independent.
- **Landmarks and spring.** Original hand-painted 2D side-scrolling game prop atlas on a genuinely transparent canvas. Exactly four isolated props arranged as a spacious 2 by 2 grid. Upper left: squat antique fruit packing weigh-station building, straight front view, amber timber wall and rugged terracotta brick base, a single small teal window and stacked empty apple crates, with rectangular flat roofline usable as a platform. Upper right: tall narrow original riveted dark aubergine cistern tower, iron stilts and detailed tall brick pillar, topped by a small teal arch-shaped operator door and warm brass bell, straight front view. Lower left: simple exposed mechanical spring with steel coil and ochre flat boot plate. Lower right: wispy diffuse low-contrast lavender cloud, painted rough paper surface, slight scattering edges, wide and flat. Warm vintage storybook gouache, cutout sprite transparency, natural irregular edges. Ample clear space between all 4 props, keep each wholly in its quadrant. No text, no characters, no scenery, no shadows outside cutouts, no gradients to opaque background.
